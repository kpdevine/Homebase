# Planet reactions — local draft

Eleven transparent PNGs used by Solar System day reactions. Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, using `../mascots/pocket-planet-blue.png` as the visual reference. Happy reuses that original mascot. The files are copied into the project; the app does not depend on the image-generation output folder.

Prompt set: one full character centered on actual alpha transparency; crisp chunky LCD pixel art, deep navy stepped outlines, striped Jupiter-like body, large readable expression, no text/sky/sticker border/blur. Keep ring arms attached to the body; remove independent orbit rings whenever the pose separates the arms from them.

| Asset | Final direction |
| --- | --- |
| Happy | Original smiling mascot with clasped ring hands. |
| Sad | Blue; wide shallow upside-down inverse of the original smile, a tear, hands resting lower together. |
| Party | Blue; party hat, gold confetti and party blower held by a ring hand. |
| Fire | Rust/orange, delighted face, triumphant raised arms, strong full yellow/orange flame halo around the whole silhouette. |
| Sun | Golden sun, dark sunglasses, relaxed smile, one casual wave and one hand at the hip. |
| Love | Rich cherry red, coral/pale pink stripes, red hearts and heart-shaped hands; keep the original Love pose. |
| Wow | Icy blue, surprised O mouth, hands raised at cheeks, gold sparkles. |
| Sleepy | Lavender, peaceful closed eyes, folded ring arms as a pillow, little gold crescent. |
| Strong | Blue, wide confident smile, two flexed biceps attached to the sides; remove all detached ring fragments. |
| Blastoff (`rocket`) | Blue, excited face, arms swept backward and golden launch trail; remove detached orbit rings. |
| Martian | Vivid green and mint striped planet with a friendly Martian face, tilted almond eyes, welcoming smile and peace sign; no separate alien sitting on the planet. |

These are reaction assets only. They do not replace the app icon, mascot, theme colors, or Home Screen artwork.
