          How scoring works

          Your daily score is the equal average of Nutrition, Gym, and To-do list. Unlogged categories leave it provisional. A Sentiments rating adds a fourth category only on days you enter one.

          Nutrition

          Your rating from 1 to 10.

          Gym

          • Workout daysChoose Yes for 4 attendance points, even with no sets. Earn the remaining 6 from sets: 4 + (goal sets + non-goal sets × 0.25) ÷ daily target × 6, capped at 10.
          • Goal sets and daily targetSets toward an unmet muscle goal get full credit; sets beyond the goal, including untargeted muscles, get 25% credit. Daily target = total weekly muscle goals ÷ 5 and stays fixed throughout that week. If all targets are zero, Gym is unscored.
          • Two flexible rest credits each weekChoose No to log rest. Your first two rest days earn 10, using one credit each; later rest days earn 0. Once every weekly muscle goal is met, further rest earns 10 without using credits. Any Yes day uses no rest credit, even if all its sets are non-goal sets. Unanswered dates stay unlogged.
          A 50-set week: daily target 10

          No sets with Yes selected: 4. Eight goal sets: 8.8. Five goal + ten non-goal sets: 8.5. Ten non-goal sets only: 5.5. A covered rest day: 10.

          Weeks always run Monday–Sunday. Sets count in date order; later workouts do not improve earlier scores. Editing historical workouts, rest answers, or targets recalculates affected scores and rest credits, and reopens changed gym reviews.

          Weekly progress is separate from daily scoring. Targets carry forward until edited. Workout suggestions prioritize the least-complete muscle goals, then the most sets remaining. Body weight is optional and never affects your score.

          To-do list

          Completed actions ÷ all actions × 10. Subtasks count individually; their parent adds no extra credit. Adding tasks alone stays unlogged. Check off an action or confirm the list review to include it. A reviewed empty list earns 10.

          While today is open, its main meter assumes remaining tasks get done. “As low as” shows the result if they stay unchecked. Calendar averages and final review use the recorded result.

          Sentiments · optional

          A 0–10 rating joins the average only when entered. Notes alone do not change your score. Confirm its review even if you choose to leave it blank.

          Logging and day review

          Log Nutrition, Gym, and To-do list to count a logged day. Rest counts, including a zero-score rest day. Confirming each section and finishing the full day review are separate steps. Score achievements require a completed day review; logging streaks only require all three check-ins.

          Days more than seven days ago are summary only.
