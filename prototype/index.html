        Daily score calculation

        Your daily score is the equal average of three sections. This is a provisional local policy you can refine as Homebase grows.

        • NutritionYour own rating from 1 to 10.
        • ChecklistThe proportion of actions checked, out of 10. Subtasks count individually; parents are not counted twice. Scheduled tasks only count after a checklist check-in. A reviewed empty list means no tasks due and counts as 10.
        • Daily training10 for logging sets or explicitly choosing a rest day. Weekly volume is tracked separately.

        Missing sections are left out of the current average and flagged. Until all three have inputs, your score is provisional. A completed day means you reviewed everything; it doesn't require a perfect score.
