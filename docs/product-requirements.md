Updated October 25, 2026. This is the current application specification; older design documents retain the planning history.

## October 25 daily reminder delivery

The reminder transport must work in Cloudflare Workers and must not follow redirects with push credentials or encrypted payloads. Use manual redirect handling, with tests covering an actual encrypted request and a rejected redirect response. Retained console diagnostics distinguish push-service acceptance from rejection/uncertainty without logging keys, subscriptions or private daily content. ## October 25 alternate workout

Training's No answer offers an optional **Log an alternate workout** button. A saved, nonblank description (up to 1,000 characters) replaces rest with an alternate workout worth exactly 5 points plus the tracked creatine point. It consumes no flex credit and adds no lifting volume or goal minutes. Users can switch back to rest or lifting; paused sets keep their IDs. Descriptions persist privately and appear in day and Training recaps. Historical changes replay weekly rest credits and reopen affected reviews. See [implementation and checks](october-25-alternate-workout.md).

## October 25 orbital meters

The approved orbital meter replaces score-ring presentation throughout the app: continuous bright fills with a dark edge and soft glow, theme-aware rails, centered passive values including decimals, and closer digit spacing for 10. Visible “out of 10” captions are removed while accessible labels retain the scale. Editable controls use a larger planet that changes expression by whole-number score band; four new retro faces mix with seven existing reactions. Passive displays omit the handle. Weekly set targets retain their 0–20 range and scale the treatment accordingly.

Nutrition ratings now accept 0–10 in all entry paths and validated backups/cloud records. Zero is logged and reviewable; blank remains unentered. Ring gestures have hard stops at each pole, cannot wrap through the bottom gap, and clear their pointer focus highlight when dragging ends. Keyboard focus and reduced motion remain supported. See [implementation and checks](october-25-orbital-meter.md).

## Purpose and delivery

Pocket Planet is a personal daily dashboard for computer, phone, and a landscape display. Development stays local. Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and classic Hosting provide optional private sync and the shared app on the free Spark plan, with no billing, Analytics, or Gemini. The production framework, display hardware, and future Spotify integration remain open.

Every source upload to `kpdevine/Homebase`, branch `codex/homebase-prototype`, must be accompanied by a deployment of the same version to Firebase Hosting. The primary custom address is `https://pocketplanet.day/`; `https://homebase-d3c6c.firebaseapp.com/` remains an accessible alias. Personal records, credentials, and local test artifacts stay out of Git and Hosting.

The custom domain uses the same existing Firebase project, Auth accounts, and owner-private Firestore records. Serve HTTPS and same-origin Google redirect sign-in on the custom domain; preserve existing sign-in behavior at Firebase/local aliases. Each browser origin retains separate local storage, and users explicitly connect the same Google account and enable sync to recover their existing cloud records. Do not force-redirect the old site or overwrite local-only records during the domain change.

## Daily records and scoring

From midnight through 12:59 a.m., Today, logging, recurring tasks, planning/editing windows, streaks, achievements, and weekly progress still use the preceding date. Keep the real local clock time and time-based greeting; show the active app date beside the clock. All weeks remain Monday–Sunday, with the new week beginning at Monday 1:00 a.m. Existing records retain their explicit date keys and UTC event timestamps.
- An open screen following Today advances after the boundary when no writing control, menu, expanded panel, drag, pending local save, or guided review is active. Preserve an in-progress interaction on its original date, then catch up when it finishes. A deliberately selected past/future date stays selected. Recheck when returning to a backgrounded app as well as during normal use. Required-section late warnings remain visible through the midnight grace hour.

- Nutrition: optional notes and a 1–10 rating. Dragging/buttons use 0.1 increments; direct entry preserves two decimal places.
- To-do list: dated one-time tasks, daily/weekday/weekly/custom recurrence, and expandable parents dependent on their subtasks. Count leaf actions once. One-time tasks precede recurring ones. Creating or scheduling tasks does not log the list; checking an action or confirming its review does. A reviewed empty list is complete.
- Task creation offers Today, Tomorrow, and an explicit date. Plan tomorrow exposes tomorrow's scheduled work without leaving the current day. Removing an occurrence supports Undo; stopping a schedule preserves history.
- The recurring manager shows only active schedules, excluding stopped/expired versions while preserving their historical records. Stopping a schedule requires a named confirmation explaining that future occurrences stop after today. Removing a recurring occurrence also requires confirmation, explicitly limited to that day; it leaves the schedule running. Cancel or closing either dialog changes nothing.
- To-do planning has three cards: TODAY, Tomorrow, and Later This Week, all with bold Kieran handwriting headers. Rename only the first planning card; Overview keeps its To-do list heading. Show the selected date instead of TODAY when a non-today date is open. At desktop width they sit side by side; phones stack and scroll normally. Later This Week groups tasks by date from two through seven days ahead, with direct day navigation and edit controls. Task creation offers a Later this week shortcut plus six date choices. New one-time tasks and routine start dates, and moving one-time tasks, are bounded to the next seven days; existing recurring schedules continue beyond that window. This is a rolling planning horizon; scoring weeks remain Monday–Sunday.
- Existing tasks and subtasks have pencil edit controls on Overview and To-do list. Edit names, add/remove subtasks, and move one-time tasks between editable dates. Keep retained action IDs and completion, recompute parents, reopen the affected checklist review, and never turn editing alone into logging. Saving an unchanged task leaves its review intact.
- Recurring editing defaults to Only this day. This day & future can change names, child dependencies, and repeat frequency from that date onward. Version the schedule so earlier saved and virtual history stays unchanged; preserve future skipped occurrences and matching completion checks. Cancel discards the draft; validation failures are atomic. Archived dates remain protected in both editor and store.
- Sentiments: optional dated handwritten notes and optional 0–10 circular rating. Notes alone are unscored. A rating, including zero, adds an equal daily-score component only when entered. Final review, history, achievements, and averages use recorded scores.
- Today and the previous seven calendar dates are editable. Older days allow summaries only; enforce this in store mutations as well as navigation.

## Gym

- Ask “Did you lift today?” Yes records attendance; No records flexible rest. Muscles: Chest, Back, Triceps, Biceps, Shoulders, Quads, Hamstrings, Core. Retain older Legs entries in history but exclude Legs from active goals.
- Deleting the last set preserves Yes and the gym attendance baseline without consuming rest credit (3.6 of the 9 gym points, plus the separate creatine point if taken). Choose No explicitly to record rest. While sets remain, explain why No is unavailable.
- Weekly muscle goals inherit until edited. Target circles use whole 0–20 sets with uniform color; Save closes the target editor. Weeks always run Monday–Sunday.
- Fixed daily target = sum of active weekly goals / 5. Useful sets fill unmet muscle goals; excess sets receive 25% daily credit. Gym component = min(10, 4 + useful-and-quarter-extra sets / daily target × 6). Scale that component to 9 points and add 1 if daily creatine is checked, then round to a tenth. Creatine applies on lifting and rest days; a missed dose is a valid, reviewable log. With all-zero set targets, only the 0–1 creatine point is available. Untouched legacy records lacking the creatine field retain their old scores and serialized shape; explicitly changing lifting/rest, sets, or creatine adopts the new policy.
- Two flexible rest credits per week earn ten each. Further rest earns zero until every nonzero muscle goal has been met; subsequent rest earns ten without using credits. Unanswered dates consume nothing. A workout with only excess sets remains a workout.
- Classify logs chronologically. Historical edits/deletions replay classifications, daily scores, and rest credits, reopening affected reviews. Later workouts do not improve earlier scores.
- Display goal sets and total sets separately, non-goal sets where relevant, and a prominent remaining rest allowance. Do not display a weighted-set total or redundant scoring descriptions.
- Suggest the ideal workout from remaining muscle deficits and the daily target, without recommending completed or disabled goals. Freeze the day's suggested muscles and set counts when Yes is selected or the first sets are logged. Keep that plan through additional logging, set removal, historical edits, target changes, reloads, and sync; progress and scoring still recalculate normally. Each new day gets a fresh plan. Older records without a saved plan derive it from the week's progress before that date, excluding the day's own sets; merely viewing a legacy record does not rewrite it. Optional daily body weight (lb/kg) is stored and visible in Training-filtered calendar history; it never changes scores.
- Make saved body weight prominent near the top of Daily training, but offer Weight graph only through Calendar → Training. On phone, keep its button fully visible below the category filters. The themed line chart has 30-day, 90-day, and all-time ranges, lb/kg conversion, latest weight and change, and an accessible values table. Use actual calendar spacing, exclude future/missing readings, and handle empty/single-point/constant series without inventing data. Derive the graph from existing day records; no extra cloud writes or schema migration.
- Log weight / Edit weight opens a compact editor; saving closes it and focuses its summary. Keep unsaved weight drafts through other training updates, label them clearly, and defer incoming cloud application until the editor closes. Closing without saving restores the saved value. Show the ideal workout before set entry, hide it on a logged rest day, fold weekly muscle gaps, and open training notes on demand or when populated.
- Hovering, focusing, or tapping a weigh-in point opens a card with that reading's date and the previous calendar day's Nutrition and Training scores using the shared circles. The previous day is literal calendar arithmetic, not the preceding weigh-in. Keep unlogged values absent. Dismiss on mouse departure, tapping away, or Escape; keep touch selections open until dismissed. Provide keyboard activation, enlarged touch hit areas, and cards constrained to the graph. Changing ranges/units clears the card.
- When enlarged point hit areas overlap, select the nearest visible dot for mouse movement or pointer selection. Preserve keyboard activation and touch pinning; ignore distant empty space.
- Show the selected date above TODAY on the To-do planning card, matching the date treatment on Tomorrow and Later This Week. All three handwritten headers share the same baseline on desktop; Overview keeps its existing heading.
